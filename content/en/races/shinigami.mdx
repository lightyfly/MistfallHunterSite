## Core Mechanic: Zanpakuto Evolution

The Shinigami race revolves around your Zanpakuto — a living spirit weapon that grows alongside you. Your progression follows a clear evolutionary path:

- **Trainee** — Starting form. Basic katana with standard sword combos.
- **Zanpakuto** — Unlocked early. Your weapon awakens with improved stats and moves.
- **Shikai** — First major milestone at Lv25+. Your Zanpakuto releases its initial true form with a unique ability.
- **Bankai** — Full Zanpakuto release. Complete the three Shikai meditation stages and conquer the **Blade Realm** challenge.

## How to Unlock Shikai

Shikai is your first real power spike as a Shinigami. Follow this route:

1. Reach **Level 25 or higher**.
2. Press the **L key** to meditate and enter your inner world.
3. Talk to your **Zanpakuto Spirit** inside the inner world.
4. Solve the **white space puzzle** and follow its visual clues.
5. Face the **Shikai Spirit Boss** in a 1v2 fight with three phases.

> The boss adds new combo strings in phase two and enters an enraged mode in phase three. Stay mobile, then punish the openings after each pattern.

## Soul Pages

As a Shinigami, you collect **Soul Pages** from defeated enemies and activities. Submit them for universal EXP when you have a surplus — the EXP scales well through mid-game.

## Division / Squad System

Shinigami can join one of the **Divisions (Squads)** within Soul Society. Each Division offers unique bonuses and access to Division-specific quests that accelerate leveling.

## Hakuda Style Training

Beyond the standard Hakuda skill tree, Shinigami players can undergo specialized Hakuda Style Training. This is an independent progression system for a dedicated fighting-style specialization.

### Pros

- Classic sword-fantasy combat with clear, readable milestones.
- Structured progression with a satisfying Shikai power spike.
- Squad bonuses add a social layer and extra stat value.

### Cons

- Bankai requires a challenging Blade Realm fight after Shikai mastery.
- Early game feels slower than a ranged Quincy route.
- The Shikai boss fight punishes players who arrive underprepared.

> Confidence note: mechanics can change with game updates. Verify the latest requirements in-game before spending rare items.
